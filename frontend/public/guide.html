VRM to MMD Converter Guide & Features

vrm2pmx-md (VRM to MMD Converter) is a free, modern web application designed to convert VRM 3D avatar models (such as those from VRoid Studio) directly into MMD-compatible PMX format right in your browser.

🔒 100% Client-Side Privacy
All conversion algorithms run locally on your device via WebAssembly. Your 3D models and textures are never uploaded to any remote server, ensuring total privacy and intellectual property safety.

✨ Key Features

🛡️
Local Processing & Security
Your model files remain strictly on your local machine. No server uploads or data retention.
👁️
Real-Time 3D Previews
Side-by-side 3D viewports let you rotate, zoom, and inspect both VRM and PMX models instantly.
💃
VMD / VPD Motion Testing
Drag and drop VMD motion or VPD pose files directly onto the converted model to preview dance animations.
📐
T/A Pose Auto Conversion
Adjust arm angles automatically to convert VRM T-poses into MMD-ready A-poses for smooth animations.
🎭
Morphs & Physics Preserved
Maps blendshapes to MMD facial morphs and generates bone hierarchies, rigid bodies, and joints.
📦
ZIP Package Export
Bundles the converted PMX and all required textures into a single, ready-to-use ZIP file.

🚀 Step-by-Step Usage Guide

Step 1

Load Your VRM Model

Drag and drop your .vrm file into the left "VRM Preview" viewport or click "Choose file" to browse.

Step 2

Inspect & Configure

Rotate and inspect the model. If desired, adjust the "T/A Pose Convert" arm angle (optimal defaults provided).

Step 3

Start Conversion

Click "Convert". The high-speed browser engine converts your model into PMX format within seconds.

Step 4

Preview & Download

Verify the result in the "PMX Preview" viewport, then click "Download ZIP" to save your MMD model package.
